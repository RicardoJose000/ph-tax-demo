Live Xero connection walkthrough

The engine connected to a live Xero organisation: authorising access with OAuth 2.0, pulling invoices and bills, and taking one transaction through to the populated BIR form. The organisation is a Xero trial; its sample Philippine invoices and bills were created through the Xero API.

What the video shows

  1. A Xero organisation connected with OAuth 2.0 (client organisations get read-only access)
  2. Syncing invoices, bills and credit notes into the normalized ledger
  3. Mapping the organisation's tax codes to Philippine tax classes
  4. Form 2303 status selecting the VAT or percentage tax path
  5. One synced invoice traced to the populated official BIR form
