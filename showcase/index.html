How it works

From ledger line
to filed figure, in five steps.

01 · CONNECT

Read the ledger

Sign in on Xero's or Intuit's own page. Invoices, bills, credit notes and expenses are pulled live, and nothing is ever written back.

02 · NORMALIZE

One ledger format

Both systems become the same ledger lines, in exact centavos, each keeping a link to its source document.

03 · ROUTE

Form 2303 decides

The registration record, not the transactions, sends each period to VAT (2550Q) or percentage tax (2551Q). Unknown tax codes are held out, never guessed.

04 · COMPUTE

Rules as data

An effective-dated rule pack with its legal references computes VAT, percentage tax and expanded withholding per ATC.

05 · FILL & TRACE

The official forms

The government's own PDF forms, filled cell by cell. Click any box to see the invoices behind it, with every step in a tamper-evident audit trail.

The output

The BIR's own forms, filled cell by cell.

No redrawn look-alikes. The engine fills the official PDF editions, and on screen every box traces back to the ledger lines behind it.

Built for accountants

The rules a careful preparer would follow.

2303

The registration record decides

VAT or non-VAT comes from Form 2303, entered by a person and effective-dated. Transaction totals never reclassify a taxpayer.

?

Unknown is held out, never guessed

A tax code with no mapping keeps its lines out of the return and raises a critical finding for review.

3M

Threshold watched, not acted on

Trailing 12-month sales against the PHP 3M VAT threshold raise an advisory for a person to decide.

#

Nothing changes silently

Registrations and the audit log are append-only, and the log is SHA-256 hash-chained, so tampering is detectable.
